This is hard to customise in Blender, not recommended if you don't know what you are doing

VERY IMPORTANT

The Texture Inside /Textures/Mixer/Lights is a 4K texture that is 21Mb! If lowered, the shadow map will look worse, but will lower to 5Mb

You can use the CDJ without the custom shader, but the lights won't work!
Maybe in the future there will be a version with another custom shader for the lights to be used with other shaders (Basic looking)

The Lightmap texture for the Mixer requires to be on UV1, not UV0
